Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

namzad ("we", "us", "our") is a marriage-focused app built for the Afghan diaspora. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

The short version: We collect what we need to run a safe, marriage-minded matching service — your profile, photos, phone number, and approximate location. We don't sell your data. We don't show ads. You can delete your account and data at any time from Settings.

1. Who we are

namzad is operated by Yusuf, a sole proprietor based in California, United States. For the purposes of the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the data controller of your personal data.

You can reach us at info@namzad.app for any privacy question or request.

2. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Phone number — used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Profile information — your name, age/date of birth, gender, photos, prompt answers, and details such as languages, faith, education, family background, and marriage intentions.
  • Verification selfie — a photo taken during verification to confirm you are a real person. This is never shown on your profile and is used only to verify you.
  • Messages — the content of conversations you have with your matches.
  • Reports and support requests — information you send us when you report someone or contact us.

Data we collect automatically

  • Approximate location — used to show you people near you and to display distance. You control this through your device's location permission.
  • Usage data — which screens you use, likes and matches, and general app activity, so we can improve the product.
  • Device data — device model, operating system version, app version, and crash logs.

Data from others

  • Apple — when you subscribe to namzad Gold, Apple confirms your purchase and subscription status. We never receive your card or payment details.

3. How we use your data

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your profile to other members and show you theirs.
  • To suggest matches based on your preferences, including age, distance, and other filters.
  • To verify that members are real people, which is central to keeping namzad safe.
  • To deliver messages between matches.
  • To investigate reports, enforce our Terms, and remove people who break the rules.
  • To provide and manage namzad Gold subscriptions.
  • To fix bugs, understand how the app is used, and improve it.
  • To contact you about your account or important service changes.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show third-party advertising in namzad.

4. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on the following legal bases:

Some profile details — such as faith or ethnic background — are treated as special category data under GDPR. We only process these where you have chosen to provide them, and you can remove them at any time by editing your profile.

5. Who we share data with

We share data only where necessary to run namzad:

  • Other members — your profile, photos, and prompt answers are visible to other members. Your phone number, verification selfie, and exact location are never shown.
  • Service providers — companies that host our servers, store photos, send notifications, and help us detect abuse. They may only process data on our instructions.
  • Apple — for subscription billing and management.
  • Law enforcement — where we are legally required to, or where we believe it is necessary to prevent serious harm.

6. How long we keep data

  • While your account is active — we keep your data so the service works.
  • If you pause your profile — your data is kept, but you are hidden from Discover.
  • If you delete your account — we permanently delete your profile, photos, matches, and messages. Deletion is completed within 30 days.
  • Limited exceptions — we may retain a minimal record where we must for legal reasons, or to prevent a banned member from returning. Records of serious safety reports may be kept longer to protect other members.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights:

  • Access — get a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — fix data that is wrong. Most of this you can do yourself by editing your profile.
  • Deletion — delete your account and data, directly in Settings → Delete account.
  • Restriction and objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — for example, by turning off location in your device settings.

If you are in the EU or UK

You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Germany this is your state's Datenschutzbehörde; in France, the CNIL; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

If you are in California

Under the CCPA/CPRA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

To exercise any right, email info@namzad.app. We will respond within the timeframe required by law (30 days under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA).

8. Security

We protect your data using encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and verification to reduce fake accounts. No service can promise perfect security, but we take this seriously and will notify you and the relevant authority if a breach affects your data as required by law.

9. International transfers

namzad is operated from the United States, so if you are in the EU or UK your data will be transferred to and processed in the US. Where we do this, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and the UK equivalent, together with additional safeguards, to protect your data.

10. Children

namzad is strictly for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we will delete it and their data immediately. If you believe a minor is using namzad, please report the profile in the app or email us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as namzad grows. If we make a significant change, we will notify you in the app or by other means before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
